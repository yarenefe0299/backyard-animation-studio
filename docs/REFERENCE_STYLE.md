# Current visual direction

The user's reference screenshots established realistic human proportions, muted
materials, directional shadows and bold white outlined captions. Their later
feedback approved that general direction but rejected the floating platform,
uncanny eyes, weak story progression, swollen shed/boards and oversized leaves.

The full revision therefore uses a continuous ground plane, neighboring houses,
background trees/hills and a visible sky. It does not expose a terrain slab edge.
The shed has structural walls, gables, thin roof planes, ridge cap, doors and trim.
Tree branches support a denser canopy of smaller shaped leaves. The garden bed,
watering can and pinned cloth use recognizable construction and scale.

The human remains the same CC0-derived mesh. Eye globes are smaller with quieter
whites, proportionate irises and neutral facial skin; brows and cheeks are not
warped for surprise. Skin classification now follows hand/finger weights instead
of painting fingers with the trouser color. A reach action and sweat show the
specific overheating problem, then relax when cooling is added.

The upgrade story follows `docs/STORY.md`. Geometry, poses, capability continuity
and export planning are tested. Visual finish and timing remain review gates for
the first full GitHub render. The source does not claim a 100% visual match to a
creator or reference from software tests alone.
