# How Hydraulic Pressure Lifts a Load

## S01 | 5.0 | pressure | hydraulics
The pump pushes oil into a closed cylinder. Pressure acts across the piston, creating a lifting force.

## S02 | 5.0 | extend | cutaway
As more oil enters, the rod extends. The supported load rises with it, without a running motor above.

## S03 | 5.0 | retract | hydraulics
Open the return path, and oil flows back to the reservoir. The rod retracts and the load settles.
