# Reference brief for your story

Replace this brief and add your own reference pictures here. The pipeline records
the reference files and their hashes; it does not automatically understand their
contents or create unseen 3D assets from them. Choose an existing modeled template
and its supported actions, or add a new template before asking for a new subject.

Keep one coherent 3D set, readable mechanical connections, smooth camera movement,
soft warm light and a clear problem/solution/payoff progression.
